# SOC Copilot — 8 Minute Judge Demo

## 0:00–0:45 — Problem
“L1 analysts spend valuable time translating raw alerts into an investigation plan, switching between tools, searching CTI, documenting evidence and preparing handoffs. The harder problem is what happens when the alert does not perfectly match a playbook.”

## 0:45–1:10 — Product thesis
**Our Copilot assists you decide what you don't yet know how to decide.**

**AI-guided. Analyst-decided.**

Explain that the product never autonomously chooses the final verdict or containment action.

## 1:10–2:00 — Smart Intake
Paste a raw Splunk brute-force alert. Show dynamic extraction of source, family, entities, severity and rule conditions.

## 2:00–3:10 — Scenario-driven Workbench
Generate the case. Highlight detailed authentication/MFA/post-login triage and Splunk-specific pivots. Contrast this with a static phishing template.

## 3:10–4:00 — CTI + evidence
Enrich the public source IP if API keys are configured. Explain source-specific results and why external reputation alone is not a verdict.

## 4:00–5:05 — Ask SOC Copilot
Ask: **“What evidence is still missing before I can decide the verdict?”**

Show the context-aware response and explicitly click **Add to documentation** or **Add as triage step**. Emphasize that the analyst accepts the action.

## 5:05–5:50 — Verdict guidance
Click **Recalculate guidance**. Show recommendation/confidence/missing evidence, then explicitly select the human verdict. Say: **“SOC Copilot recommends; the analyst owns the verdict.”**

## 5:50–6:50 — Knowledge creation
Create an SOP from the current investigation. Apply a client profile. Show detailed investigation steps, pivots and escalation criteria. Download as PDF/DOCX.

## 6:50–7:30 — Product extensibility
Open **Integrations**. Show the 14-connector catalog, server-side secrets, read-only-first design and custom connector draft model.

## 7:30–8:00 — Operational continuity
Show **Shift Handoff** and **Audit Logs**. Close with:

“From raw signal to defensible human decision, reusable knowledge and shift continuity — one analyst workspace.”
